# MindPulse — Mental Wellbeing Analytics System

MindPulse is a data-driven mental wellbeing analysis tool that transforms simple lifestyle survey data into meaningful insights about stress, burnout risk, and overall wellbeing patterns. It is designed for students, professionals, and institutions to better understand early warning signs of mental strain — not as a diagnostic tool, but as an awareness and decision-support system.

---

## 1. Problem Statement

Mental wellbeing challenges are often **undetected until they become severe**. Most existing solutions are either too clinical, too general, or reactive instead of preventive.

In schools, workplaces, and communities, there is currently **no simple, accessible system that uses everyday lifestyle data to identify early signs of stress, burnout, or poor mental wellbeing**.

### MindPulse solves:
> How can we use self-reported lifestyle data (sleep, stress, screen time, and social habits) to detect early mental wellbeing risk patterns and make them understandable and actionable?

## 3. Insights & Key Findings

MindPulse goes beyond visualization — it reveals **hidden behavioral patterns**.

### Key Insights
- High screen time combined with low sleep strongly correlates with higher risk levels.

- Users often underestimate their own stress levels compared to behavioral indicators.

- Low social support is a consistent predictor of negative wellbeing outcomes.

- Small lifestyle adjustments (especially sleep improvement) significantly reduce risk scores.

### Key Takeaway
Mental wellbeing is not random — it follows **predictable lifestyle patterns** that can be measured and acted upon early.
