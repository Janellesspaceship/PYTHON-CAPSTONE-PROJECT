### MindPulse : A Mental Health Trend Analyzer for Early Detection and Intervention.

`Turning Emotional Data into Early Support`


1. What is the problem you are solving?

`I am solving the problem of hidden and poorly understood mental health struggles among teenagers and youth by using data to uncover trends, triggers and opportunuties for early intervention.`



2. What tools did you use?

`Python` - Core logic for data processing, scoring system, and application structure.

`Pandas` - Cleaning, transforming, and analyzing survey responses.

`NumPy` - Numerical calculations for wellbeing risk scoring.

`SQLite3` - Local relational database used to store and persist user survey data.

`Plotly` - Interactive dashboard.

`Matplotlib` - Main tool for generating trend charts and risk visualizations.

`VS Code` - Full capstone build.




3. What insights did you or do you want to discover?/ What Solutions do you want to offer? Do People even need these solutions?

`INSIGHTS`
- Users with **high screen time + low sleep consistently fall into higher risk categories**.

- Stress levels reported by users often **do not match behavioral indicators**, showing self-awareness gaps.

- Lack of social support strongly correlates with higher wellbeing risk scores.

- Small lifestyle improvements (especially sleep consistency) can significantly reduce risk levels.

- Mental wellbeing is **predictable through lifestyle patterns**, not random.

- Users often underestimate their own risk until data is visualized.

- Early warning signals exist long before burnout becomes visible.

- Simple survey data is enough to generate **meaningful behavioral insights**.


`SOLUTIONS`
- Personalized wellbeing feedback per user.

- Risk classification system (low / moderate / high risk).

- Anonymous trend analysis for communities.

- Actionable recommendations such as:
    - Improving sleep habits.

    - Reducing screen time.

    - Managing stress levels.

    - Strengthening social support systems.


`IMPORTANCE`
- `Schools need it`: Teachers often see perfomance drops but not emotional causes.

- `Parents need it`: Many parents miss early warning signs.

- `Youth need it`: Many struggle silently due to stigma.

- `NGOs/Government need it`: Policy without data is often ineffective.

- `Real world needs it`: Mental health struggles are often invisible until crisis.
